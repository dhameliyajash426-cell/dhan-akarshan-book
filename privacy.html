Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

To process your digital purchases and deliver orders reliably, BLIX ENTERPRISE may collect the following information:

  • Customer mobile number / WhatsApp number
  • Order ID and transaction reference number
  • Payment status and timestamp
  • Purchase history and download access records
  • Basic technical information required for website functionality (such as browser type and device characteristics)

2. How We Use Information

Customer information is used strictly for legitimate business and transactional purposes, including:

  • Processing orders and digital purchases
  • Verifying payments with authorized payment gateways
  • Delivering digital products and access links
  • Providing prompt customer support
  • Preventing fraudulent or unauthorized transactions
  • Maintaining transaction and accounting records

3. Payment Security

All payments are securely processed through Cashfree Payments or other RBI-authorized payment providers.

🔒 Zero Sensitive Financial Data Stored: We do NOT store customers' UPI PINs, bank account credentials, credit/debit card numbers, or CVV security details on our servers. All financial processing is handled entirely by Cashfree's PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade customer personal information.

Information may only be shared with authorized payment processors, secure cloud hosting providers, and technical service providers strictly when necessary to operate our website or fulfill digital product purchases.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable and modern administrative, physical, and electronic security measures to safeguard customer information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

6. Data Retention

Customer transaction information may be retained for legitimate business, accounting, tax, legal, and regulatory compliance purposes for the duration required by applicable Indian laws.

7. Customer Privacy Rights

Customers may contact us regarding their personal data, transaction queries, or privacy-related requests, subject to applicable laws.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically when required. The latest version will always be posted on this page with immediate effect.
